Zan Gilani

Hello! I’m Zan (pronounced like but not short for Zanzibar) and I work on improving educating by combining psychology, games and tech.

I’m a a Director of Product at Duolingo, where I co-created Video Call, a way to practice speaking foreign languages with conversational AIs. I now co-lead the 35-person org that works on making Video Call more fun and more effective.

Before Video Call, I spent almost a decade at Duolingo working on user growth (including the new user experience, retention, and Asia).

Recently, I started Royal Jellies, a series of taste portraits of curious people.

I was born in London, grew up in Karachi, and live in NYC’s oldest Chinatown (my favourite neighborhood in my favourite city). These days, you can find me playing soccer and jianzi, researching urban design, volunteering at Elizabeth Street Garden, and trawling the depths of Tiktok and Wikipedia.

Want to chat? Come grab 20 minutes in my open hours. Otherwise, email me here: hi@zangilani.com or dm me on Instagram.
